    Summary

    As a seasoned maritime lawyer with cognitive years of experience, he specialises in Shipping and Maritime law,456 Cargo Claims and Disputes, Litigation and Arbitration. His expertise spans contract review, litigation, arbitration, or regulatory compliance.

    He represents shipowners, charterers, cargo interests, or insurers in Nigeria but the scope of his practice is not bound by jurisdictions. He represents the interest of our clients in the various maritime countries, globally. He aims to provide practical, effective, and efficient legal solutions to help clients navigate the complexities of maritime law.
